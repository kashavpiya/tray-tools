# Changelog

## v0.1.0

First Windows x64 executable release.

- Mouse Battery displays a Logitech wireless mouse's battery percentage through G HUB.
- Audio Switcher toggles between two outputs with one click.
- Audio Switcher opens a device picker on first launch and offers **Choose audio outputs...** in its tray menu.
- Both apps offer **Start when I sign in** from the tray menu.
- Packaged apps store settings and status in separate folders under `%LOCALAPPDATA%\TrayTools`.
- Includes a Windows build script, dependency license notices and SHA-256 checksums.

Tested on Windows 11 x64 with a PRO X SUPERLIGHT 2, Realtek speakers and SteelSeries Sonar Gaming. The executables bundle Python 3.14.7. They are unsigned. Windows 10 and other hardware have not been independently tested.
